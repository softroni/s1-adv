# S1-ADV 0.2.215

Air Music is a new game. Plug the M5Stack I2C ultrasonic unit into the Grove port, open Air
Music from Games or type `/music`, and wave a hand over the sensor to play notes. Closer is
higher. It has ten notes, 4 cm apart, shown as a rainbow of bars.

The notes are a pentatonic scale, so anything a child plays sounds good. A held hand holds
the note, and playing keeps the screen on. The sound stops while the assistant is using the
mic or speaker, and while the menu is open.

The Games menu holds eight items, so BrainFlow moved to Focus.
