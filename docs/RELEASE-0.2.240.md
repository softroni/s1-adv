# S1-ADV 0.2.240

In short: Eye and pet apps removed

Chat Eyes, Big Eyes and AI Pet are gone. The AI Agent tab now holds Realtime, Pulse and AI Chat.

Everything that opened them went too: E and F in the Ctrl+K menu, Ctrl+E and Ctrl+F, `/face`,
`/pet`, `/hero`, `/tama` and `/petreset`, and the pet actions Claude could send. Ctrl+K is shorter
now, and `chat` opens Full Chat when something asks for that screen by name.

The pet no longer ticks or scans Wi-Fi in the background, so the Wi-Fi settings screen can't be told
the pet is busy scanning.

Realtime keeps its eyes and animals, and Big Eyes stays as a screensaver style, so neither looks any
different. If a screensaver started on the Realtime screen, waking now goes to Home. It used to land
on the bare eye screen.

A saved pet stays in the device's settings storage, unused, and comes back if you roll back.
