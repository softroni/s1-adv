# S1-ADV 0.2.251

In short: More pages for every part

Most parts in the Lab now have more than one page. Dots under the title show where you are, and left
and right turn the page, or **N** on the Servo and Speaker.

- **Ultrasonic:** a parking sensor, with beeps if you want them
- **ENV III:** a comfort map of the room
- **Joystick 2:** draw on the screen, and a target game
- **Mini Angle:** a steady hand game
- **Light:** a sky that follows the reading
- **PIR motion:** a guard alarm that gives you 5 seconds to leave
- **Tilt:** a spirit level, and a ball to roll into a hole
- **Servo:** ready-made moves at three speeds
- **Speaker:** a piano on the number keys

Most sensors also get a full graph of the last 30 seconds, scaled so small changes show.

Press **D** on Devices for the Dashboard: every live sensor at once, with its last minute.

The arrows in the Lab's key hints now draw too. They'd been missing.
