# S1-ADV 0.2.216

Flappy Bird is a new game. Hold a hand over the ultrasonic unit on the Grove port and the
bird follows it, from 6 cm at the bottom of the screen to 30 cm at the top. Take your hand
away and the bird falls. Space or any other key flaps instead, so it also plays with no
sensor. Open it from Games or type `/flappy`.

The pipes speed up and the gaps narrow as the score rises. The best score lasts until the
device restarts.

The Games menu holds eight items, so KeyRush moved to Focus, next to BrainFlow.
