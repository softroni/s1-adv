# S1-ADV 0.2.211

Snake now fills the screen. The snake and food are 10 pixels wide instead of 6, the board
is 24 by 13 squares, and the edges of the screen are the walls.

All text is gone from the game, including the score. A still snake is dimmed, and a lost
one has a red head. The controls haven't changed: WASD or the arrow keys steer, Enter starts,
pauses or begins a new game, N restarts, and Esc leaves.
