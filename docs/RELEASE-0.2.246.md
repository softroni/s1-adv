# S1-ADV 0.2.246

In short: Call rules by name

Ask for something like "when I say movie time, turn the strip blue", and you get a rule that listens
for its name. On the Playing screen, tap Alt and say "movie time" to switch it on, and say it again
to switch it off.

This only needs your words, so it skips Claude and answers in under a second. Rules wait while it
listens, and what's on stays on.
