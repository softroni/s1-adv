# S1-ADV 0.2.242

In short: Change rules by talking

Pick a rule in the list, tap Alt and say what should be different, like "make it stay on longer" or
"only when it's dark". Claude sees the rule as it is now and sends it back, with what changed
standing out. Enter replaces the old rule, E walks through it in the steps, and Esc keeps what you had.

Voice tools can now tell Claude more than the words. In the Lab that's what's plugged in, the
readings and your saved rules.

G0 and Ctrl+V on a Lab screen now go to the Lab, not the chat.

**The bridge needs a restart for this.** Until then, it shows "Restart the bridge to use this."
