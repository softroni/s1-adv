# S1-ADV 0.2.244

In short: Set parts by voice

On the Servo, LED strip, Cardputer LED and Speaker screens, tap Alt and say "set it to 90", "make it
blue and a bit brighter" or "a bit more". Your hands can stay on the parts.

A spoken setting only moves the part whose screen is open. Relays, the lamp and the air conditioner
keep to their keys, because speech can be misheard and they can switch mains.

**The bridge needs a restart for this.** Until then, it shows "Restart the bridge to use this."
