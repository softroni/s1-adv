# S1-ADV 0.2.226

Ball Catch is harder. The ball starts 50% faster and falls faster. It now keeps some of its
slide as it drops, so you have to aim ahead of it, and gusts push it around at higher levels.

Black bombs appear after your first two catches. Let them fall past the basket, because
catching one costs a heart. A ball can't wait forever either: the rail runs out, and then the
ball drops by itself.

Every 5 catches the basket shrinks, bombs get more common and the rail gets shorter.
