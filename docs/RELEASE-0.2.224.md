# S1-ADV 0.2.224

Ball Catch is a new game. A ball slides along the top of the screen, the Joystick 2's button
drops it, and the stick moves a basket underneath to catch it. Space drops and A and D move
on the keyboard, so it plays with no joystick too. Open it from Arcade or type `/catch`.

The ball speeds up as you score, up to three times its starting speed, and the basket shrinks
every 5 catches. Each ball is a new color, and its shadow shows where it will land. A miss
costs one of three hearts.

Every menu was full, so there's a new Arcade group for the sensor and joystick games. Air
Music, Flappy Bird and Hot Air Balloon moved there from Games. The launcher's number keys now
go up to 8.
