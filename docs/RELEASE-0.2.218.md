# S1-ADV 0.2.218

Hot Air Balloon is a new game for children. Connect an ENV III and a Joystick 2 to the Grove
port through a hub, open it from Games or type `/balloon`, and blow on the ENV III to heat
the balloon. The joystick steers. Its button, or Space and W on the keyboard, also fire the
burner, and A and D steer, so it plays with no sensors at all.

Collect stars and avoid the birds. There are three hearts, floating hearts give one back,
and the ground is soft, so landing never hurts. Every 10 stars the sky changes from day to
sunset to a starry night, and the wind picks up.

The Games menu holds eight items, so Chess moved to Focus, next to Chess Mind.
