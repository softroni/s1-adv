# S1-ADV 0.2.243

In short: Ask the Lab anything

Tap Alt on Devices, on a part's screen or on the wiring screen, or open **Ask the Lab** at the bottom
of the rules list. Claude sees what's plugged in, the live readings and your rules, so you can ask
"why didn't my rule fire?" or "why won't the strip light up?" and get an answer about your own setup.
It shows on the screen and is read aloud.

Ask "what can I make with these parts?" and you get up to three rules to choose from. Left and right
move between them, Enter saves one, and Esc drops them all.

**The bridge needs a restart for this.** Until then, it shows "Restart the bridge to use this."
